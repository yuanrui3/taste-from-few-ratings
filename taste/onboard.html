Rate the person's appearance 0–10. Keys: 0–9, T = 10, Backspace = back.

Your taste in 40 ratings

You rate 40 people, chosen to cover the whole pool; it takes about 4 minutes. The model starts from a crowd face-beauty score (from public research ratings of studio faces, read through two kinds of face codes) and leans on your ratings more as you give them (after 20, half crowd and half you; after 40, two thirds you), then ranks everyone you did not rate.

Forty is where each extra rating stops paying much. You can stop earlier with Show predictions; 20 is the useful minimum.

Your ratings stay in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
